# Department Discovery Template: Procurement Department

This discovery document gathers the vendor management rules, request workflows, and evaluation models needed to build the **Procurement** module in the EF Enterprise Management Platform.

---

## 1. Procurement Lifecycle
*   What is the standard step-by-step lifecycle of procurement (from Requisition to Purchase Order and Delivery)?
*   How are lead times tracked for different material categories?

## 2. Vendor Registration
*   What documents are required for vendor registration (e.g. trade license, tax certificates, qualification levels)?
*   How are vendor classifications organized (e.g., Manufacturer, Supplier, Contractor, Consultant)?

## 3. RFQ (Request for Quotation) Workflow
*   How are RFQs created, and how are vendors selected to receive them?
*   How are vendor bids submitted and tracked (e.g. portal submission, email, physical bid box)?

## 4. Quotation Comparison & Evaluation
*   What criteria are used to build quotation comparison summaries?
*   Is there a scoring matrix for technical compliance, financial offer, and delivery times?
*   How are exceptions or variations in quotations handled?

## 5. Purchase Approvals
*   What is the authorization matrix for purchase requisitions and Purchase Orders (PO)?
*   Specify monetary thresholds for different roles (e.g. Procurement Officer, Procurement Manager, DGM).

## 6. Reports & Metrics
*   **Supplier Scorecard:** Delivery speed, rate compliance, quality scores.
*   **Purchase Register:** Summary of active and historical POs.
*   **Savings Tracker:** Budgeted price vs. actual purchase price.

## 7. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
*   **Purchase Cycle Time:** Average days from requisition to PO release.
*   **Supplier Lead Time Deviation:** Promised delivery date vs. actual delivery date.
*   **Requisition Rejection Rate:** Percentage of requisitions returned for corrections.
*   **Cost Savings Percentage:** Measure of financial savings achieved.

## 8. Dashboard Requirements
*   *Procurement Officer View:* Active requisitions, pending RFQs, bid deadlines, and PO tracking.
*   *Manager View:* Master supplier lists, purchase volume by project, and KPI performance scorecards.
