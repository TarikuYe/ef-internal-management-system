# Department Discovery Template: Office Engineering

This discovery document gathers the operational workflows, quantity tracking methods, and calculation requirements to build the **Office Engineering** module in the EF Enterprise Management Platform.

---

## 1. BOQ Preparation Workflow
*   What is the process for preparing a Bill of Quantities (BOQ)?
*   How are standard item codes, descriptions, units of measurement, and quantities structured?
*   How are variation order items handled in relation to the original contract BOQ?

## 2. Estimation Workflow
*   How are initial cost estimations calculated for bidding or project scoping?
*   What parameters are used (e.g. historical price indexes, supplier rates, machinery logs)?
*   How are contingency allowances computed?

## 3. Quantity Verification
*   How are physical site measurements verified against drawing details?
*   What is the workflow for generating and approving Payment Certificates (payment certificates / take-off sheets)?
*   Who is responsible for the site measurement approval?

## 4. Cost Calculations
*   What mathematical formulas are used for rate analysis?
*   Are price adjustments or escalations factored in? If so, what indexes are used?

## 5. Approval Hierarchy
*   Who approves cost estimations and BOQ items (e.g., Office Engineer, Senior Quantity Surveyor, Head of Office Engineering)?
*   What are the monetary limits for approvals?

## 6. Reports Generated
*   **Monthly Payment Certificate (MPC):** Summary of works completed and approved amounts.
*   **Take-off Sheet:** Detailed dimension calculations.
*   **Variance Report:** Budgeted vs. actual quantities and costs.

## 7. Existing Spreadsheets
*   Attach sample templates of the Excel sheets currently used for take-off calculations, rate analysis, and MPC summaries.

## 8. Dashboard Requirements
*   *Engineer View:* Active project take-offs, calculation review statuses, and assigned estimations.
*   *Manager View:* Master project budget tracker, cost variance summary, and payment certification schedules.
