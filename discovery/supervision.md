# Department Discovery Template: Supervision & Water Works

This discovery document gathers the field logs, inspection requirements, and progress tracking inputs required to build the **Supervision & Water Works** module in the EF Enterprise Management Platform.

---

## 1. Daily Site Workflow
*   What parameters do field engineers log daily (e.g. weather conditions, labor headcounts, active machinery, site instructions, safety logs)?
*   How are photos and site snapshots attached?

## 2. Inspection Request (IR) Process
*   What is the lifecycle of an Inspection Request?
    1.  **Request:** Filed by contractor.
    2.  **Scheduling:** Assigned to supervisor.
    3.  **Site Inspection:** Done physically.
    4.  **Result Logging:** Pass / Fail / Comments.
*   Are signature records or digital signs required on site?

## 3. Material Tracking
*   How are incoming site materials verified against specifications (e.g., concrete test cubes, steel reinforcing bars, pipe materials)?
*   How are quality testing logs and test reports attached and tracked?

## 4. Delay Reporting & Claims
*   How are unforeseen delay factors recorded (e.g. heavy rainfall, material shortage, access issues)?
*   How does the site supervisor feed information into the EOT (Extension of Time) process managed by Contract Administration?

## 5. Mobile & Field Access
*   Since field engineers work on site, is mobile access required for inspections, photo uploads, and daily logs?
*   Are offline capabilities needed if site connectivity is limited?

## 6. Reports Generated
*   **Daily Site Progress Report:** Activity details, labor, and weather log.
*   **Weekly Inspection Summary:** Requests approved and rejected.
*   **Material Quality Register:** Summary of material tests and approvals.

## 7. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
*   **Schedule Variance (SV):** Planned progress vs. actual progress.
*   **Inspection Pass Rate:** Ratio of approved to total inspections.
*   **Safety Incident Rate:** Number of safety incidents logged.

## 8. Dashboard Requirements
*   *Supervisor View:* Pending inspection requests, daily site logs, test schedules, and action items.
*   *Manager View:* Master site progress index across all active supervision contracts, health index charts, and pending safety alerts.
